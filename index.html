About

I am an aspiring economist applying to PhD programmes in Economics, with research interests at the intersection of international trade, firm productivity, and knowledge spillovers. My work asks how knowledge travels between firms embedded in global value chains, and what determines whether exposure to a foreign trading relationship becomes lasting capability or simply passes through once the relationship ends.
